You need senior tech leadership. You don't need a $300K salary on your payroll. Enter the fractional CTO.

## The Problem

Most SMEs hit a technology ceiling. The founding team makes early tech decisions, but as the business grows, those decisions accumulate into technical debt, security gaps, and missed opportunities.

You need someone who can:
- Evaluate your technology landscape
- Set technical strategy and architecture
- Build and mentor your engineering team
- Make vendor and tool decisions
- Ensure security and compliance

But a full-time CTO costs $200K-$400K annually — before equity. For most SMEs, that math doesn't work.

## What a Fractional CTO Actually Does

A fractional CTO works with your business part-time — typically 10-20 hours per month. They provide:

**Strategic Direction**
Architecture decisions, technology roadmaps, and build-vs-buy analysis. The big decisions that compound over years.

**Team Development**
Hiring strategy, code review standards, mentorship, and engineering culture. Building a team that can execute independently.

**Technical Due Diligence**
Security audits, performance reviews, vendor evaluations. Protecting your business from invisible technical risks.

**Execution Support**
Sprint planning, technical project management, and hands-on coding when needed. Not just advice — actual results.

## When to Hire a Fractional CTO

- You're spending more than $10K/month on development but have no technical leadership
- You're planning to raise funding and need technical credibility
- You've been burned by outsourced development gone wrong
- Your development team is growing past 3-4 people
- You're considering a major technology migration or rebuild

## The ROI

The math is straightforward. A fractional CTO at $5K-$15K/month prevents six-figure mistakes, accelerates delivery by 30-50%, and builds a team that doesn't depend on any single person.

The cost of *not* having senior tech leadership is always higher — you just don't see it until it's too late.
