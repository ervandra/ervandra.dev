AI isn't just for enterprises with deep pockets. Small and medium businesses can implement AI automation today — with the right strategy and realistic expectations.

## Start With the Pain Points

Don't start with the technology. Start with the problem. Ask yourself:

- Where does your team spend the most time on repetitive tasks?
- What processes are most error-prone?
- Where are the bottlenecks in your operations?

These are your AI opportunities.

## Three Low-Cost Entry Points

**1. Customer Support Automation**

AI chatbots and email responders have matured significantly. Tools like custom GPT agents can handle 60-80% of common customer inquiries, freeing your team for complex issues.

**2. Document Processing**

Invoice processing, data extraction from forms, contract review — these are perfect candidates for AI automation. The ROI is immediate and measurable.

**3. Internal Knowledge Base**

Turn your scattered documentation into an AI-powered knowledge assistant. Your team gets instant answers instead of searching through folders.

## The Budget-Friendly Approach

You don't need a six-figure budget. Here's a realistic path:

1. **Week 1-2:** Audit your processes and identify 3 automation candidates
2. **Week 3-4:** Pilot the highest-impact, lowest-risk automation
3. **Month 2-3:** Measure results, iterate, and expand

Most SME AI projects can start under $5,000 and show ROI within the first month.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Trying to automate everything at once
- Choosing technology before defining the problem
- Ignoring change management — your team needs to adopt it
- Not measuring before and after metrics
